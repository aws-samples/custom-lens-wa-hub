# AIDLC Best Practice Lens
AIDLC Best Practice Lens provides best practices for adopting the AI-Driven Development Life Cycle (AI-DLC). It organizes 18 questions into 4 custom pillars: Trustworthy, Auditable, Scalable, and Standardized.

| Pillar | Questions | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trustworthy | 5 | Human oversight, AI test generation and validation, sensitive information and tool access, AI review of artifacts, agent autonomy |
| Auditable | 3 | Traceability from business intent to production, records of AI interactions and human decisions, evidence of testing and deployment |
| Scalable | 6 | Parallel AI-driven delivery, context management across teams and sessions, delivery capacity and cost, product definition, CI/CD pipelines, operations and incident triage |
| Standardized | 4 | Shared AI tooling and AI-DLC workflows, organizational engineering standards, consistent artifacts and completion criteria, AI-assisted development as a team practice |
